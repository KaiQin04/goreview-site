Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings → tap "Restore Purchases". Your GoReview Premium unlock will be restored automatically if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

How does AI analysis work?

GoReview uses KataGo running entirely on your device via Apple's CoreML framework. No internet connection is required for analysis. The AI engine uses your device's Neural Engine (ANE) for fast inference.

How do I import games from OGS?

Go to Settings → OGS section → tap "Connect OGS Account". After authorizing, your completed games will sync automatically.

How does iCloud sync work?

When iCloud sync is enabled, GoReview automatically backs up your game records, analysis results, and settings to your iCloud account. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, your data will be restored automatically.

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes all features with two limits: up to 300 games in your library and up to 10 AI-analyzed games per month. Gameplay, import/export, iCloud sync, and OGS integration are all fully available.

What does GoReview Premium unlock?

Premium is a one-time purchase that removes all limits: unlimited game library and unlimited AI analysis. All other features are identical.
