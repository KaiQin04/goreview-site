Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

GoReview is a local-first Go (Baduk/Weiqi) game review app. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite:

  • Game records (SGF files)
  • AI analysis results
  • User preferences and settings

No data is sent to any server operated by GoReview.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your game records, analysis results, and settings are backed up to your personal iCloud account using Apple's iCloud infrastructure. This data is encrypted by Apple and only accessible with your Apple ID. GoReview does not have access to your iCloud data.

OGS Integration

GoReview optionally connects to the Online Go Server (OGS) to import your game records. This requires OAuth authorization through OGS. When connected:

  • Your OGS OAuth credentials (access token) are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device
  • GoReview fetches your completed game records from the OGS public API
  • No OGS data is sent to GoReview servers

You can disconnect your OGS account at any time from the Settings page.

AI Analysis

All AI analysis is performed on-device using KataGo with Apple CoreML. No game data is sent to external servers for analysis.

In-App Purchases

GoReview offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase (GoReview Premium). Purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. GoReview does not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

GoReview does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Clipboard Access

GoReview can read your clipboard when you explicitly tap the "Import from Clipboard" button, to detect SGF game data for import. Clipboard content is only read on your explicit action and is never transmitted or stored beyond the import process.

Children's Privacy

GoReview does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app does not require account creation or personal data input.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at 98stoned.thrifty@icloud.com or visit our support page.
